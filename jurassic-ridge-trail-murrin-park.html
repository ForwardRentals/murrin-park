The Jurassic Ridge Loop

A short, scrambly loop above Browning Lake with two viewpoints over Howe Sound. Here's what to expect, how long it takes, and how to fit it into a Murrin day.

Murrin Park is small, and the Jurassic Ridge loop is the perfect size for it: about an hour on a rocky little trail that climbs above Browning Lake to two big views over Howe Sound.

The numbers

  • Distance: about 2 km, as a loop.
  • Elevation gain: roughly 150 m.
  • Time: around 1 hour, give or take viewpoint stops.
  • Difficulty: easy by distance, but steep and rocky in places.
  • Dogs: on leash.

What it's like

This is not a groomed path. Expect roots, granite slabs, short steep pitches and a bit of easy scrambling where you might use your hands. It's the same rock the climbers are on below you, so it can be slick when wet. In return you get two viewpoints looking out over Howe Sound, the highway and the fjord, which feel far bigger than an hour's walk deserves.

What to wear and bring

  • Shoes with grip. Trail runners or hikers. Flip-flops are a bad idea on granite.
  • Water, especially on hot days; the rock radiates heat.
  • A camera. The viewpoints are worth it.

Safety

  • Stay back from edges at the viewpoints. There are drops.
  • Wet granite is slippery. Take it slowly after rain.
  • Watch kids closely on the scrambly sections.
  • Stay on the trail. Shortcuts erode the thin soil on the rock.

When to go

Spring through fall is best. In winter and early spring the rock can be wet, mossy or icy. In summer, go early: the lot can fill before 10 a.m. on hot weekends, and the morning light on the Sound is lovely.

Make a Murrin day of it

The loop pairs perfectly with the rest of the park:

  1. Walk the Jurassic Ridge loop first, while it's cool.
  2. Watch climbers on the walls (from a respectful distance; see our note on crag etiquette).
  3. Swim or paddle Browning Lake. Squamish Canoe Rental delivers canoes and boards to the lot.

That's a satisfying half day. More ideas in our Murrin Park day trip plan.

Questions

How long is the Jurassic Ridge trail?

It's a short loop of about 2 km with roughly 150 m of elevation gain. Most people take around an hour, including stops at the viewpoints.

Is the Jurassic Ridge loop good for kids?

Older kids who are comfortable on rocky, uneven ground usually enjoy it. Some sections are steep and scrambly, so keep a close eye on younger children near the viewpoints.

Can you bring dogs on the Jurassic Ridge trail?

Dogs are allowed on leash in Murrin Park. Some rocky steps can be awkward for small or older dogs.
